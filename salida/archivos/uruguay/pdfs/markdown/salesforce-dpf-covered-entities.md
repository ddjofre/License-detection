# **Salesforce Data Privacy Framework Covered U.S. Entities** 

Last Updated: May 18, 2026 

Salesforce complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (collectively, the “Data Privacy Framework” or “DPF”) as set forth by the U.S. Department of Commerce. For additional information about Salesforce’s DPF commitments, please see the “International transfers of Personal Data” section of the Salesforce Privacy Statement. 

For purposes of Salesforce’s DPF certification, Salesforce, Inc. and Salesforce-controlled U.S. subsidiaries and entities identified in Salesforce’s self-certification submission are covered entities and adhere to the applicable DPF Principles with respect to Personal Data transferred to the United States in reliance on the DPF. Covered entities include the following: 

Apromore, Inc. Cimulate, Inc. Demandware, LLC Heroku, Inc. Krux Digital LLC Mulesoft, LLC OwnCompany Inc. Quip LLC Salesforce, Inc. Slack Technologies, LLC Tableau Software, LLC 

A broader list of Salesforce’s global affiliates can be found in the “List of Subsidiaries” section of Salesforce’s most recent Form 10-K, available under the <u>SEC Filings</u> tab by selecting the “Annual Filings” filter. 

This list may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Salesforce’s covered U.S. entities, services, corporate structure, or certification scope. To view Salesforce’s DPF status, please visit the official DPF participant search page.
